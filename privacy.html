call-transcriber Privacy Policy

call-transcriber is a personal tool used only by its owner.

  • It accesses only the owner's own Google Drive, through Google OAuth, to download audio files and upload transcript text files.
  • Data is processed only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with, or sent to any third party.
  • No data from any other Google user is collected.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
